# Project Proposition

This document contains the project proposition for a Minecraft mod featuring the Moomin universe and its characters.

**IMPORTANT: This document mainly servers as scratchwork for a more detailed requirements specification in the future. The concrete details will be worked out later.**

## Core Concept

Moomins are very cute and huggable, and it would be quite nice to somehow bring them into our familiar Minecraft worlds. The mod that is the subject of this document aims to do just that.

In addition to adding new blocks, items and mechanics that are not found in the vanilla game, the mod will incorporate some linear or nonlinear narrative elements inspired by the Moomin universe. This is not to say that the mod will have a "story mode" of sorts, it's more like a set of quests/advancements and events in connection with the main characters of the universe.

The mod will mainly focus on exploration-related content instead of tech, magic or automation commonly found in other popular mods.

Below, you can find a set of examples of what the mod will bring to the game. This list is a first draft, yet to go through a MOSCOW process, so it may change wildly in the future. Sorry for that :P

## Examples for New Content

### Blocks

These can be grouped into two big categories.

**Decorative blocks:** Purely decorative items inspired by the Moomin universe and its characters.

**Functional blocks:** These blocks will provide new functionality or mechanics not found in the vanilla game. These functionalities will, of course, **offer some advantage or reward** for the player.

### Items

Items fall into many categories within Minecraft. Adding new items in each category is within the scope of the mod, I guess :P.
I will list a couple examples.

**Food items:** Moomin food :3 This can range from items that simply refill your hunger bar to more valuable ones that might give temporary status effects.

**Tools:** Items that can be used to accomplish some task in the world. These can be enhanced versions of vanilla items, or something completely new.

**Equipment:** Items that the player can wear. Some obvious examples would be Moominpappa's top hat or Snufkin's hat. Ideally, these are not easy to obtain, and provide some sort of advantage to the player.

### Structures

Some structures should appear once per world (e.g. the Moominhouse itself), while others are randomly generated all across the map.

Some examples of this are Moomin villages or Hattifattener islands.

### Mobs

Apart from generic Moomin or Snork NPCs, the mod will feature many other creatures from the Moomin universe. For instance, Hattifatteners, dragons or ancestors (a.k.a. monke).

Whether you receive anything other than sadness when killing (murdering?) them is anyone's guess at this point :P

Unique named mobs based on the main characters are also featured. They will are listed below.

### Named Characters

The following characters will most likely be featured in the mod in some way:

- Moomintroll
- Moominpappa
- Moominmamma
- Little My
- Snufkin
- Snorkmaiden
- The Groke

The list is non-exhaustive. Who knows what the future will bring? :P

### Quests and Events

Names (or, potentially, unnamed) characters will sometimes have a task for the player. These might range from obtaining or crafting a specific item, building a predefined multiblock structure or traveling to a certain location within the world.

These quests or events are ideally inspired by stories from within the Moomin universe.
